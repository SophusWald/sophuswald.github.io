Graph coloring

A k-coloring of a graph gives each vertex one of k colors so that no edge joins two vertices of the same color. Choose k and a number of vertices, and you get a graph. Half of the time it can be colored with k colors (but not with fewer), and half of the time it cannot be colored with k colors at all. Your task is to find a k-coloring, or to decide that none exists.

Choose a color, then click a vertex to paint it; clicking it again with the same color removes the color. You can also press the keys 1–k to pick a color and 0 for the eraser. Drag vertices to move them around. Edges whose endpoints have the same color are shown in red.

Deciding whether a graph is 2-colorable is easy (check that it has no odd cycle), but for every k ≥ 3, deciding whether a graph is k-colorable is NP-complete.
