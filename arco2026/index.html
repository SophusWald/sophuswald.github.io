About

ARCO (Algorithmic Research Cooperation around Øresund) is a one-day meeting for people working on algorithms in the Øresund region. It takes place once or twice a year and moves between universities on both sides of the strait. This year it is hosted in Copenhagen. Everyone interested in algorithms is welcome, including students.
