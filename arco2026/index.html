About ARCO

ARCO (Algorithmic Research Cooperation around Øresund) is a recurring one-day meeting for researchers in algorithms in the Øresund region. It is held once or twice a year, with the host institution rotating between universities in the region. The meeting is an informal venue for presenting recent work and meeting colleagues from neighbouring groups. Students are very welcome.

Previous editions include ARCO 2024, ARCO 2023 and ARCO Spring 2016.
